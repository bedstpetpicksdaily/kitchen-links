Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Bee's Wrap reusable food wraps last?

With proper care and hand washing, Bee's Wrap products typically last 1-3 years of regular use. The lifespan depends on frequency of use and washing habits. When they eventually wear out, they're fully compostable.

Can I use Bee's Wrap wraps with hot foods?

Bee's Wrap wraps work best with cool to room-temperature foods. Avoid direct contact with temperatures above 75°F (24°C) as excessive heat can compromise the beeswax coating. Allow hot foods to cool before wrapping.

Are Bee's Wrap products certified organic and sustainable?

Yes, Bee's Wrap uses certified organic cotton and sustainably sourced beeswax. The company is committed to ethical sourcing and zero-waste packaging, making them an excellent choice for environmentally conscious consumers.

How do I clean and care for my reusable food wraps?

Simply hand wash your wraps in cool water with mild soap immediately after use. Air dry completely before storing. Avoid hot water and dishwashers, which can damage the beeswax coating. Proper care ensures maximum lifespan.

Will Bee's Wrap wraps work with all container sizes?

Bee's Wrap offers various sizes and their bowl covers with elastic edges fit most standard bowl and container sizes. The moldable nature of beeswax wraps makes them adaptable to different shapes and dimensions.
