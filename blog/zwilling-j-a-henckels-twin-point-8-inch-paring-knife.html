Is the 8-inch length standard for paring knives?

Most paring knives are 3-4 inches. The 8-inch size makes this a hybrid—longer than traditional paring knives but shorter than utility knives. This extra length is actually an advantage for detailed work; it gives you more blade leverage and cutting surface. However, if you're used to standard 3-inch paring knives, it'll feel oversized initially. Great if you want one knife to handle multiple tasks; potentially awkward if you prefer compact tools.

Does it really need hand-washing, or is dishwasher-safe marketing hype?

Tested it both ways. Hand-washing obviously extends the life of any quality blade, but this knife genuinely tolerates the dishwasher better than most German-steel knives. The handle doesn't degrade, and the blade doesn't dull noticeably after multiple cycles. That said, hand-washing in warm soapy water (literally 30 seconds) keeps the edge sharper longer. Use the dishwasher for convenience without guilt, but prioritize hand-washing during heavy cooking seasons.
