Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Bee's Wrap products last?

With proper care and washing in cool water with mild soap, most Bee's Wrap products last 1-2 years or longer. The beeswax coating gradually wears with use, but the wraps remain effective throughout their lifespan before becoming fully compostable.

Can I use Bee's Wrap in the microwave?

Bee's Wrap is not recommended for microwave use, as high heat can damage the beeswax coating. However, you can use it to cover bowls before microwaving or warm food gently with the wrap still attached for short durations.

Are Bee's Wrap products safe for all foods?

Yes, Bee's Wrap is food-safe and made from organic cotton and sustainably sourced beeswax with no harmful chemicals. However, they're best for dry and fresh foods—avoid prolonged contact with raw meat or hot foods.

How do I clean and maintain Bee's Wrap?

Rinse Bee's Wrap in cool water with mild soap immediately after use and air dry. Avoid hot water and dishwashers as heat can compromise the beeswax coating. Store in a cool, dry place or hang on hooks.

Is Bee's Wrap compostable?

Yes, when your Bee's Wrap reaches the end of its life, it's completely compostable in home or commercial compost facilities. The organic cotton and beeswax break down naturally without leaving harmful residue.
