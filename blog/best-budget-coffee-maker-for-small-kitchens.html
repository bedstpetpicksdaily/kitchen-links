What to Look For

  • Compact Footprint: Look for models with a width under 8 inches and a depth under 10 inches. Vertical designs and single-serve brewers naturally take up less counter space than traditional 12-cup drip machines. Measure your actual available space before shopping to ensure the appliance fits between cabinets or against your wall.
  • Multi-Function Capability: Since space is limited, consider coffee makers that do more than one thing. Combo units that brew both single-serve K-cups and traditional coffee, or models that heat water for tea, give you flexibility without requiring multiple appliances cluttering your kitchen.
  • Removable or Hidden Reservoir: Models with detachable water tanks or tanks that sit behind the brewer save significant counter space. Avoid machines with large external reservoirs that jut out prominently, as they create visual clutter and take up more square footage than necessary.
