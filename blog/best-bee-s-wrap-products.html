Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Bee's Wrap products actually last?

With proper care, Bee's Wrap typically lasts 1-2 years of regular use. Hand wash with cool water and mild soap, then air dry flat. Avoid direct heat and extreme temperatures to extend the lifespan.

Can you use Bee's Wrap with hot foods?

No. Bee's Wrap is designed for room-temperature and cold foods only. Heat will melt the beeswax coating and compromise the wrap's effectiveness. Always allow food to cool completely before wrapping.

Are Bee's Wrap products truly plastic-free?

Yes, Bee's Wrap uses organic cotton fabric coated with beeswax, plant-based resins, and tree resin. The wraps contain no plastic or synthetic materials, making them fully compostable at end of life.

What sizes does Bee's Wrap offer?

Bee's Wrap offers several sizes including small (7x8 inches), medium (10x11 inches), and large (13x14 inches). They also sell assorted multi-packs and specialized products like bread storage bags and large bowl covers.

Is Bee's Wrap worth the price compared to plastic wrap?

While initial cost is higher, Bee's Wrap saves money long-term by eliminating the need for disposable plastic wrap purchases. Over 1-2 years of use, the wraps pay for themselves while reducing environmental impact significantly.
