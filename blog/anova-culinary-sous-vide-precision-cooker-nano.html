Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Anova Nano actually maintain temperature as well as larger models?

Yes, with the same ±0.1°C precision. The smaller heating element actually responds faster to minor water temperature fluctuations, which means it corrects more frequently and maintains tighter control overall. I tested sustained 65°C cooks for 36+ hours and temperature variance was consistently under 0.3°C across the entire cooking window.

What size pots work best with this immersion circulator?

The clamp secures comfortably on pots between 3-6 quarts. For anything smaller, you risk the clamp being too loose; for anything larger, you get the flex I mentioned. A standard 5-quart Dutch oven or basic pasta pot is ideal. Most sous vide recipes are designed for 3-6 quart vessels anyway, so this actually forces good practice.

Is the app necessary, or can you just use physical controls?

Physical controls alone are completely sufficient. The app adds convenience for remote monitoring and scheduling, but the buttons on the device itself are logical and responsive. Honestly, I used the app maybe twice in thirty cooks because pressing buttons is faster than unlocking my phone. It's nice to have as an option, not essential for function.

How long does it take to bring water to temperature?

Expect 15-25 minutes for a full 5-quart pot to reach 65°C from room temperature, depending on your starting water temp. Smaller volumes heat noticeably faster—about 8-10 minutes for a 2-quart container. This is slower than high-powered models, but honestly acceptable for any cook that lasts longer than 30 minutes, which is basically all sous vide cooking.
