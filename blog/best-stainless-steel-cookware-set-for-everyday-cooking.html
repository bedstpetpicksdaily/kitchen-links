What to Look For

  • Tri-ply or Clad Construction: Look for cookware with aluminum or copper cores sandwiched between stainless steel layers. This ensures even heat distribution across the entire pan, preventing hot spots that burn your food. For everyday cooking, tri-ply construction is the sweet spot—it's affordable without sacrificing performance.
  • Comfortable, Secure Handles: You'll be reaching for these pans multiple times daily, so handles matter immensely. Choose sets with riveted stainless steel handles or heat-resistant silicone grips that won't loosen over time. Handles should be angled slightly for better control when pouring or maneuvering heavy pans.
  • Versatile Piece Selection: For everyday cooking, prioritize sets that include 8-inch and 10-inch skillets, a 3-quart saucepan, and an 8-quart stockpot. Skip overly large sets with pieces you'll rarely use—a focused collection of essential pans is more practical and easier to store than a massive 12-piece set.
