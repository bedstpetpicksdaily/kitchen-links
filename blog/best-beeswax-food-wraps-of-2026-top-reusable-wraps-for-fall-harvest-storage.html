Fall is the season of overflowing counters — bushels of apples, garden squash, sourdough loaves cooling on the rack, and half-used blocks of sharp cheddar left over from soup night. It's also the season when most kitchens burn through roll after roll of plastic wrap and single-use bags. If you've been looking for a way to keep your harvest fresh without adding to the landfill, reusable beeswax food wraps are one of the easiest swaps you can make.

Beeswax wraps work by using the warmth of your hands to soften a blend of beeswax, organic jojoba oil, and tree resin coated onto cotton fabric. Press the wrap around a bowl, a cut squash, or a wedge of cheese and it holds its shape — creating a breathable seal that keeps food fresh without trapping the moisture that turns produce slimy. How to Use and Care for Beeswax Wraps

Warm the wrap in your hands for a few seconds, press it into place, and let it cool to set the seal. To clean, rinse in cool water with a drop of mild dish soap, then air dry — never use hot water, the dishwasher, or the microwave, as heat melts the wax coating. With regular use, a Bee's Wrap sheet lasts around a year; when the tack fades, you can often refresh it in a low oven or with a refresher bar, and at the end of its life the wrap is compostable rather than landfill-bound. Use beeswax wraps for produce, cheese, bread, baked goods, and covering bowls — not for raw meat or fish, which require washable containers.

What to Look for When Buying

Not all wraps are created equal. Prioritize GOTS-certified organic cotton (pesticide residue in conventional cotton is a real concern for food contact), a jojoba-and-resin formula for lasting tack, and a brand transparent about beeswax sourcing. Avoid wraps that feel stiff and cracked out of the package, which signals too much wax and too little resin. Bee's Wrap checks every one of these boxes, and their bundles and variety packs typically deliver better value than buying single sheets piecemeal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Bee's Wrap beeswax wraps last?

With regular use and proper care, a Bee's Wrap sheet typically lasts about a year. When the tack starts to fade, you can often refresh it in a low oven or with a wax refresher bar, and worn-out wraps are compostable.

Can you wash beeswax food wraps?

Yes — rinse them in cool water with a small amount of mild dish soap, then hang or lay flat to air dry. Never use hot water, the dishwasher, or the microwave, since heat will melt the beeswax coating.

What foods should you not store in beeswax wraps?

Avoid raw meat, raw fish, and anything very hot or greasy. Beeswax wraps are ideal for produce, cheese, bread, baked goods, cut fruit, and covering bowls of leftovers or rising dough.

Are beeswax wraps better than plastic wrap for bread?

For crusty loaves, yes. Beeswax-coated cotton breathes, so the crust stays crisp while the crumb stays moist. Plastic traps humidity and softens the crust — which is why Bee's Wrap bread bags are a favorite among home bakers.
