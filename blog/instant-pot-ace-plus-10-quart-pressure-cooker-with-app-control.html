Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app work without WiFi, and what happens if your internet goes down?

The app requires WiFi for remote control, but the Ace Plus functions perfectly as a standard pressure cooker using the physical buttons on the unit itself. You lose remote access if WiFi drops, but cooking continues uninterrupted. The device remembers your settings and reconnects automatically once your network is back online.

How long does a full 10-quart batch actually take from start to finish?

Cooking time depends heavily on what you're making. A full batch of pulled pork takes roughly 90 minutes total (20 min preheat, 60 min pressure cook, 10 min natural release). Soups and stews typically finish in 45-60 minutes. The app timer feature is genuinely helpful here because you can watch the countdown from anywhere instead of hovering over the kitchen.

Is this better than a regular 6-quart Instant Pot for meal prep?

The 10-quart makes sense if you're cooking for 8+ people or meal prepping 4+ days of portions in one session. A 6-quart requires two cooking cycles for the same output, adding roughly 45 minutes to your total prep time. If you're prepping twice weekly anyway, the 10-quart cuts that to once weekly—that's 4+ hours saved monthly. For 2-4 person households, the 6-quart is honestly more practical and takes up less counter space.
