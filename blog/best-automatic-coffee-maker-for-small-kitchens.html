What to Look For

  • Compact Footprint: Look for machines with a width under 10 inches and a depth under 8 inches. Vertical designs that stack rather than sprawl across your counter are ideal—think tall and narrow rather than wide and bulky.
  • Under-Cabinet or Corner Placement: Choose a coffee maker with a low profile (under 12 inches tall) that can tuck under your cabinets, or select one specifically designed to fit snugly in corner spaces without blocking window sills or outlets.
  • Dual-Purpose Features: Since every inch matters, prioritize machines that offer brewing flexibility—like models that brew both single cups and full pots, or machines with built-in grinders that eliminate the need for a separate appliance.
