Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Bee's Wrap beeswax wraps last?

With proper care, Bee's Wrap wraps typically last up to one year. Their durability depends on frequency of use and following care instructions (hand washing in cool water, avoiding direct heat).

Can you use beeswax wraps in the freezer?

Yes, Bee's Wrap wraps work in the freezer, though the beeswax coating becomes slightly stiff. They perform optimally at room temperature to cold temperatures, making them perfect for refrigerated storage.

Are Bee's Wrap products vegan-friendly?

Bee's Wrap uses beeswax, so they're not suitable for vegans. However, the brand is transparent about ingredients, and some competitors offer plant-based alternatives if that's important to your gift recipient.

How should I care for my Bee's Wrap wraps?

Hand wash in cool water with mild soap immediately after use. Air dry completely before storing. Avoid hot water and direct heat from stovetops or ovens, as this can damage the beeswax coating.
