Frequently Asked Questions

What temperature should my Big Horn Outdoors pizza oven reach for optimal cooking?

Big Horn Outdoors pizza ovens are designed to reach temperatures between 800-900°F for authentic Neapolitan-style pizzas. Most recipes require around 850°F for perfectly charred crusts in 60-90 seconds. Allow 30-45 minutes of preheating to achieve optimal temperature stability before cooking.

Can I use a Big Horn Outdoors pizza oven for cooking dishes other than pizza?

Absolutely. Big Horn Outdoors pizza ovens excel at roasting vegetables, whole chickens, breads, and desserts. The intense dry heat creates beautiful caramelization on autumn vegetables like squashes, peppers, and mushrooms while infusing them with subtle wood smoke flavors.

How do I maintain my Big Horn Outdoors equipment during harvest season?

Regular maintenance includes brushing grates after each use, checking for debris in the pizza oven dome, and ensuring proper ventilation around your equipment. Cover your Big Horn Outdoors gear during heavy rain or when not in use for extended periods to protect the finish and ensure longevity.

What fuel works best for Big Horn Outdoors pizza ovens?

Seasoned hardwoods like oak, maple, and fruitwoods provide the best results for Big Horn Outdoors ovens. Avoid softwoods and treated lumber. Store wood in a dry location, and allow it to season for at least 6 months before use for optimal burning and flavor development.

How many people can I cook for with a Big Horn Outdoors pizza oven and grill setup?

A complete Big Horn Outdoors setup easily serves 20-40 people depending on your menu. Multiple cooking zones allow simultaneous preparation of appetizers, mains, and desserts, making large harvest gatherings manageable and enjoyable.
