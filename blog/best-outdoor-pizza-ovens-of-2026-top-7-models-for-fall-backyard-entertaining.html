What Makes a Great Outdoor Pizza Oven in 2026

The pizza oven market has matured fast, and the spec sheet matters more than the marketing. Look for three things above all: peak temperature (you want 800–950°F for true Neapolitan crust), recovery time (how quickly the stone reheats between pies — critical when you're feeding eight people), and insulation quality (double-walled stainless steel holds heat far better in 45°F fall weather than a single-skin shell). Fuel type is the next decision: propane for convenience and control, wood pellets for smoky flavor, or a multi-fuel design that lets you switch depending on the night. Finally, consider portability — if you're hauling the oven to tailgates or campsites, weight and folding legs suddenly matter a lot.

Fall Entertaining Tips for Pizza Oven Owners

Cold-weather cooking changes the math slightly. Preheat 5–10 minutes longer than the manual suggests, keep the oven out of direct wind, and stage your dough at room temperature indoors so it doesn't stiffen up. Prep toppings in advance in small bowls so guests can build their own — a pizza oven turns a party into an activity. And invest in a good infrared thermometer plus a turning peel; those two accessories do more for crust quality than almost any upgrade. Frequently Asked Questions

Can you use an outdoor pizza oven in cold fall or winter weather?

Yes. Insulated stainless ovens like those from Big Horn Outdoors work well in cold weather — just add 5–10 minutes to your preheat time, shield the oven from wind, and keep the door or opening covered between pies to hold temperature.

Is gas or wood better for an outdoor pizza oven?

Gas offers faster startup and precise temperature control, making it ideal for weeknights and beginners. Wood or pellets deliver authentic smoky flavor and higher ceiling temps. A multi-fuel Big Horn Outdoors oven lets you choose based on the occasion.

How long does a pizza take to cook in an outdoor pizza oven?

At 850–950°F, a thin Neapolitan pizza cooks in 60 to 120 seconds. You'll need to rotate the pie every 20–30 seconds with a turning peel for even browning.

Do Big Horn Outdoors ovens come with a pizza stone included?

Most Big Horn Outdoors pizza oven models ship with a cordierite baking stone included. Replacement stones, covers, peels, and burner accessories are also available directly from the brand.
